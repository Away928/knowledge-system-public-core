# Scope and excluded external resources

This repository only includes local **Academic Dashboard** and **Cognitive Engine** application code, tests, protocols and anonymized configuration examples.

- `PyYAML` and `pytest` are external Python dependencies installed separately under their respective licenses; they are not bundled.
- Obsidian and associated plugins are not bundled or relicensed.
- Learning Hub content, student/course materials, paywalled or licensed lecture documents, audio/video media, third-party datasets and private personal knowledge are not included.
- Any AI/LLM providers, browser integrations and user's existing vault are external and must be configured separately.
